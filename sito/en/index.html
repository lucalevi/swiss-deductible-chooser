    The rule

    Why almost only two deductibles ever matter

    Three numbers, nothing else

    You pay twelve premiums. You pay everything yourself up to the deductible. Above it you pay 10%, but never more than 700 francs a year. The total cost of a year is the sum of those three things — there is nothing else to it.

    The middle loses

    Between 300 and 2,500 there are four more deductibles. Do the arithmetic and almost none of them ever wins: their cost curve stays above one of the two extremes at every level of spending. That is why Insurek was built — and the chart shows it.

    And then there is the insurer

    The right deductible is half the money. The other half is that for benefits the law makes identical, the gap between the dearest and the cheapest insurer in your area can be over a thousand francs a year. Insurek works out both at once.
